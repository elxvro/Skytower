## Level rewards above Level 6

For every claimable level `L >= 7`, use this exact four-level repeating cycle based on `(L - 7) % 4`:

- `0`: 125 coins
- `1`: 1 Slow Time power-up
- `2`: 150 coins
- `3`: 1 Wide PERFECT power-up

A level reward can be claimed exactly once.
